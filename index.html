Our Story

Two hearts, one beautiful promise

The Love God Wrote in His House

We never imagined that the place where we both went to seek God would also be the place where He would lead us to each other.

We were members of the same church, but for a while, we worshipped in different branches—completely unaware that the person God had prepared for each of us was already part of the same church family.

Then came the day when our paths finally crossed.

She moved to his branch, and what seemed like an ordinary change became the beginning of something extraordinary.

Our first real conversation was simple and unexpected. She had heard that he was unwell and reached out to check on him. Neither of us knew that a simple message of concern would become the first chapter of our love story.

What began as a conversation slowly became friendship. Friendship grew into genuine care, constant communication, and eventually, a love neither of us had planned for.

For him, it was her heart—her kindness, sincerity, and love for God—that stood out. For her, it was his character, his humility, his love for God, and the way he treated people that made her see something special in him.

We didn’t fall in love overnight. We discovered each other gradually. We learned each other’s hearts, dreams, weaknesses, and strengths. And somewhere along the way, we realized that what we had was more than friendship—it was something God was building.

Our relationship taught us that love is not simply about finding someone we are attracted to. It is about finding someone with whom we can pursue God, grow together, serve together, laugh together, and build a life centered on Christ.

There were moments of uncertainty and seasons when we wondered what God was doing. But looking back, we now understand that every delay had a purpose. God was preparing not only our relationship, but also our hearts for the covenant we were about to enter.

He brought us together in His house, at His appointed time, and in His own beautiful way.

Today, we look back and realize that none of it was accidental—not the different branches, not the move, not the illness, not that first message, not the friendship, and not the journey that followed.

Every detail was part of a story God had already written.

We came to church seeking God.

He gave us each other.

And now, as we stand together as bride and groom, we know that this is more than a love story.

It is a testimony of God’s timing, God’s purpose, and God’s grace.

People may ask, “How did you meet?”

Our answer will always be:

“We met in church. But God found us first.”

And perhaps the most beautiful part of our story is knowing that the same place where we learned to love God became the place where God taught us how to love each other.
